# Suitcase-Car
A modern version of the 1991 Mazda Suitcase Car
This project is a suitcase which unfolds into a go-kart, modeled after the Mazda 1991 design. Although modeled after Mazda’s mine differs in a few major places. The cost to build the original Suitcase Car was quoted at $5,000, while mine has a cost of ~$850, an 83% price reduction. The original Suitcase Car was a gas powered vehicle making it incredibly impractical for taking on flights due to the flammable liquid that is needed to power, while my design is electric, centered around an E-bike battery. My favourite car brand is Mazda and one of my biggest passions is automotive engineering, a field I want to go into, and my first idea for Hackclub was making a modern version of the Honda Motocompo. After finding out that there already is a modernized motocompo I went for its “competitor” in the Mazda Suitcase Car. 
The idea for using an electric scooter as the steering system comes from the youtuber "Bucket".
